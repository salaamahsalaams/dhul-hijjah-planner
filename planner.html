Your 10-Day Journey

6 Essential Reminders

Your guide to making the most of these blessed days

1

Avoid Sins

As much as good deeds are more rewarded, sins are more despised by Allah during these days. If you commit sins, you will be deprived of His mercy.

  • Protect your eyes, ears, tongue, hands, and heart
  • Do not listen to the whispers of Shaytan
2

Obligatory Worship

Establish your obligatory acts of worship — they are the foundation upon which everything else is built.

  • Pray five times a day on time
  • Guard your salah with khushu'
3

Fasting

Fasting is highly recommended during these days. Fast especially on the Day of Arafah (9th Dhul Hijjah) and as many of the first 8 days as you can.

  • Day of Arafah fasting expiates the sins of the year before and the year after
  • No day when Allah frees more people from Hellfire than Arafah
4

Du'a & Dhikr

Not a single moment should pass by when you're not remembering Allah. Wake up for tahajjud and spend long hours communicating with Him.

  • There is no du'a better than the du'a made on Arafah
  • The Prophet ﷺ and his companions would increase their dhikr
5

Sacrifice

If you make your intention to sacrifice, be mindful of not trimming your hair or nails as soon as Dhul Hijjah starts until your sacrifice has been performed.

  • No hair trimming from 1st Dhul Hijjah
  • No nail clipping until sacrifice is done
6

Lock In

It's time to finish off this year becoming the best version of ourselves as Muslims. An animal is not enough.

  • Slaughter your ego, anger, corruption, lies, bad behavior, and sinful desires
  • Minimize social media & distractions
