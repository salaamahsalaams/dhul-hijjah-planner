6 Essential Reminders

Your guide to making the most of these blessed days

1

Avoid Sins

As much as good deeds are more rewarded, sins are more despised by Allah during these days.

  • Protect your eyes, ears, tongue, hands, and heart
  • Do not listen to the whispers of Shaytan
2

Obligatory Worship

Establish your obligatory acts of worship — they are the foundation.

  • Pray five times a day on time
  • Guard your salah with khushu'
3

Fasting

Fast especially on the Day of Arafah and as many of the first 8 days as you can.

  • Arafah fasting expiates sins of the year before and after
  • No day when Allah frees more people from Hellfire
4

Du'a & Dhikr

Not a single moment should pass by without remembering Allah.

  • No du'a is better than the du'a made on Arafah
  • The Prophet ﷺ and his companions increased dhikr
5

Sacrifice

If you intend to sacrifice, don't trim your hair or nails from 1st Dhul Hijjah.

  • No hair trimming from 1st Dhul Hijjah
  • No nail clipping until sacrifice is done
6

Lock In

Finish off this year becoming the best version of ourselves. An animal is not enough.

  • Slaughter your ego, anger, lies, and sinful desires
  • Minimize social media & distractions

Begin with sincerity and intention

Day 8 — Yawm al-Tarwiyah

The Day of Quenching — pilgrims head to Mina
  • Pray all 5 salawat on time
  • Fast today (last day before Arafah)
  • Recite Dhikr 1 — Subhanallah... x100
  • Recite Dhikr 2 — La ilaha illallah... x100
  • Tahajjud — prepare spiritually for Arafah
  • Read Quran
  • Plan your Arafah day — clear your schedule
  • Make lots of istighfar
9

Day 9 — Yawm al-Arafah ARAFAH

The greatest day of the year
Fasting on this day expiates the sins of the year before and the year after. There is no du'a better than the du'a made on Arafah. Allah frees more people from Hellfire on this day than any other.
  • FAST — the most important fast of the year
  • Pray all 5 salawat with deep khushu'
  • Recite Dhikr 1 — Subhanallah... x100+
  • Recite Dhikr 2 — La ilaha illallah... x100+
  • Spend LONG hours making du'a
  • Cry to Allah — beg for forgiveness
  • Du'a for yourself, your family, the Ummah
  • Read Quran — especially Surah Al-Ikhlas
  • Make du'a before iftar — it is not rejected
  • Tahajjud the night before — do not miss it
10

Day 10 — Eid al-Adha EID

Celebrate and glorify Allah
  • Eid Mubarak! Do NOT fast today
  • Pray Eid salah
  • Recite Takbirat: Allahu Akbar, Allahu Akbar...
  • Perform your Qurbani (sacrifice)
  • Dress well and celebrate with family
  • Give to those in need
  • Make du'a of gratitude to Allah
  • Continue your dhikr — don't stop now
